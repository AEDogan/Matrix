VetAssist Application Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At VetAssist ('we', 'our', or 'the App'), we prioritize the absolute privacy and data sovereignty of veterinary practitioners, clinics, and their clients.

1. Zero Cloud Tracking & 100% Local Storage

VetAssist operates on an Offline-First architecture. All medication databases, price lists, stock inventories, customer logs, and clinical calculation parameters are stored strictly locally on your device (via LocalStorage/SQLite/Internal Storage). We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your clinical billing data on external cloud servers.

2. Permissions Used by the Application

VetAssist requests only minimal, necessary system permissions to execute core offline functions:

  • Storage / Media Access: Required solely to save and export generated JPG adisyon/receipt image slips to your device so they can be shared via WhatsApp or printer.
  • Internet / Network (Optional): Used only when the user explicitly triggers a manual Google Sheets CSV inventory synchronization. No personal data is sent.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer any user data, veterinary records, or client phone numbers to any third parties or advertising networks.

4. Contact and Support

For privacy questions, feature requests, or clinic onboarding, please contact us at: support@vetassist.app.
