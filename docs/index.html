Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about closed testing, offline support, and Google Sheets.

Does VetAssist work without internet in remote villages? +
Yes, absolutely! VetAssist is built with an offline-first architecture. All calculation engines, custom medication formulas, and visual receipt generation work completely offline.
How do I join the Google Play closed testing track? +
1. Join our official Google Group (vetassisttesters) with your Google Play account.
2. Click the Google Play Opt-in Link and click 'Become a Tester'.
3. Download VetAssist directly from Google Play Store!
How does Google Sheets synchronization work? +
Publish your Google Sheet as a public CSV link and paste it into the VetAssist Settings. Tapping 'Sync Stock' instantly refreshes all prices and stock counts.
Are my patient and client data stored on external servers? +
No. All records, treatment logs, and prices reside exclusively inside your device's local storage for complete medical privacy.
